Our History

A Small Idea From Home, Inspired By Generations. VarnaKala was founded in 2020, not in a large factory, but as a small business from home with a big vision. For generations, our family has been dedicated to the art of traditional fabric design. We took that paramparic heritage and developed it to suit the modern world. That passion for colors and art is the reason we named it "VarnaKala" - The Art of Colors. Our main purpose for starting this journey was simple to provide our people with a comfortable, trustworthy, and fast service to buy high-quality clothing easily. We wanted to make premium T-Shirts and Polo Shirts accessible to everyone, everywhere.

What started as a small home-based idea has now grown. Today, we are proudly serving customers from our main branch in Pasyala and our other branch in Galle, with island-wide delivery across Sri Lanka. Our mission is to take this trusted service we started to every corner of the country. Thank you for being a part of our journey.
